# Cravitoo · Vendor Registration Checklist

*Please read this document carefully before starting your registration with Cravitoo. Keeping every document ready will let us activate your account within 24–48 hours.*

**Contact:** onboarding@cravitoo.com  ·  **Website:** https://app.cravitoo.com

---

## 1. Business & Ownership Details

Before you begin, please have the following business details ready:

- [ ] Registered legal name of the business
- [ ] Trade / brand name (name that appears on packaging and menus)
- [ ] Type of business (Proprietorship / Partnership / LLP / Pvt Ltd / OPC)
- [ ] Date of incorporation
- [ ] Full registered address (with pincode)
- [ ] Operating kitchen address (if different from registered address)
- [ ] Owner / authorised signatory name
- [ ] Owner PAN number
- [ ] Owner Aadhaar number (optional, only for KYC verification)
- [ ] Primary contact person name
- [ ] Primary contact phone (10-digit, active WhatsApp preferred)
- [ ] Primary contact email
- [ ] Secondary contact phone (for order emergencies)
- [ ] Company website / social handles (if any)
- [ ] Cuisine type (Indian / Chinese / Continental / Bakery / Beverage / Multi-cuisine)

---

## 2. Statutory Documents (Mandatory)

Please keep **clear, legible PDF or JPEG scans** (max 5 MB per file) of the following documents ready to upload:

- [ ] **GST Certificate** — Registration certificate showing your 15-digit GSTIN. If turnover is below the threshold, please upload a declaration of GST exemption.
- [ ] **PAN Card** — Business PAN (not personal PAN for Pvt Ltd / LLP entities).
- [ ] **FSSAI Licence** — State / Central Food Safety and Standards Authority of India licence covering the kitchen address. **Must be valid for at least the next 90 days.**
- [ ] **Shop & Establishment Certificate** — Municipal / labour-department registration for the operating premises.
- [ ] **Cancelled Cheque** OR **Bank Passbook Front Page** — showing the account number, IFSC, and account holder name that matches your business.
- [ ] **Bank Details** — Filled bank-details form (or use our template) confirming:
   * Bank name
   * Account holder name (must match GST / PAN name)
   * Account number
   * IFSC code
   * Branch name
   * Account type (Current / Savings)

---

## 3. Optional but Recommended

Uploading these will speed up approval and unlock premium vendor benefits:

- [ ] **MSME / Udyam Certificate** — priority listing for MSME-registered vendors
- [ ] **Public / Product Liability Insurance** — minimum ₹5 Lakh cover recommended
- [ ] **Hygiene / Food Safety training certificates** for kitchen staff
- [ ] **ISO 22000 / HACCP** certification (if any)
- [ ] **GSTIN of secondary kitchens** (if you serve multiple sites from separate kitchens)

---

## 4. Menu Preparation

Before completing the Menu tab of onboarding, please prepare:

- [ ] Full menu list with the following per item:
   * Item name
   * Short description (max 200 characters)
   * Category (Starter / Main / Beverage / Dessert / Combo / …)
   * Price in ₹ (inclusive of GST — no hidden charges)
   * Vegetarian / Non-vegetarian flag
   * Meal period(s) available: Breakfast / Lunch / Snacks / Dinner (choose one or more)
   * High-resolution photo (optional but strongly recommended — 800×600 px or larger)
- [ ] Signature dishes / bestsellers marked separately
- [ ] Any items that are seasonal or available only on specific days
- [ ] Combo meals with itemised inclusions
- [ ] Special dietary items (Jain, Gluten-free, Diabetic-friendly, Keto, …)

You can upload the menu one of two ways during onboarding:

1. **Bulk Excel upload** — download the template from the Menu tab and fill it in.
2. **Item-by-item form** — add each dish manually via the "Add item" button.

**Excel column format:** `name, category, price, description, meal_period, is_vegetarian, is_available, image_url`
*(meal_period accepts comma-separated values, e.g. `lunch, snacks`)*

## 6. Commercial & Legal Terms (to be agreed with Cravitoo)

Please review and be ready to accept the following during registration:

- [ ] **Commission percentage** on the invoice value (typical range 12–18 %, discussed with our onboarding manager)
- [ ] **Payment cycle** — Weekly / Bi-weekly / Monthly settlement
- [ ] **Payment channel** — Bank transfer / UPI
- [ ] **Return & refund policy** — vendor's obligation on incorrect / delayed / spoilt orders
- [ ] **Non-solicitation clause** — you will not solicit Cravitoo's customers directly for the duration of the agreement
- [ ] **Food-safety indemnity** — vendor indemnifies Cravitoo against any FSSAI / hygiene claims from end-customers
- [ ] **Digital signature on Vendor Onboarding Agreement** — a scanned copy on your company letterhead or an e-sign via DigiLocker / eMudhra
- [ ] **Cancelled cheque uploaded** with signature and stamp

## 9. Registration Steps (What Happens Next)

1. **Fill Basic Details** — you (or the Cravitoo onboarding manager) fill in Section 1 of this checklist on the vendor onboarding form.
2. **Upload Documents** — attach the scans listed in Section 2 (and optionally Section 3).
3. **Site Review** — the Cravitoo site-admin verifies documents, may request corrections. You'll receive email notifications for every status change.
4. **Menu Upload** — you enter or upload your menu via the Menu tab.
5. **Site Visit** — a Cravitoo representative visits your kitchen.
6. **Master Approval** — a Cravitoo master admin reviews the full application and either approves or requests changes.
7. **Activation** — on approval, you receive a welcome email with your **Vendor App** login credentials. Your menu goes live at the linked corporate sites.

**Typical timeline:** 24–48 working hours if all documents are correct on the first submission.
